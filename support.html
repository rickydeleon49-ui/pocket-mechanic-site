Support

Pocket Mechanic is a car logbook with an optional OBD-II scanner for iPhone.

Which adapters work?

Bluetooth Low Energy OBD-II adapters based on ELM327, such as Veepeak OBDCheck BLE+ and vLinker MC+. Classic Bluetooth and Wi-Fi adapters are not supported on iPhone.

My adapter doesn't show up

Turn the ignition on, check that Bluetooth is on, and allow Bluetooth for Pocket Mechanic in iPhone Settings. Don't pair the adapter in iPhone Settings; connect from the app's Scan tab.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone only. Make a backup from Settings > Backup and keep the file in Files or iCloud Drive.

A recall is listed that doesn't match my car

The list covers the model and year. Use the official VIN check link to confirm your car.
