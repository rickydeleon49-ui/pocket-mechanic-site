Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Pocket Mechanic keeps your car records on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your cars, VINs, service and fuel records, reminders, receipt photos, saved pages and scan results are stored only on your iPhone. We cannot see them. A backup file goes only where you choose to save or send it.

What the app sends, and to whom

The app talks directly to public government services. It does not send anything to a Pocket Mechanic server.

  • VIN lookup: the VIN you enter is sent to NHTSA's vPIC service to decode the car.
  • Recalls, complaints and safety ratings: the car's make, model and year are sent to NHTSA.
  • Fuel economy: the car's make, model and year are sent to fueleconomy.gov.
  • Manual and how-to links: pages you open are loaded from the site they belong to.

These services receive your request like any website does, under their own privacy policies.

Bluetooth and camera

Bluetooth is used only to talk to your OBD-II adapter when you start a scan. The camera is used only when you scan a VIN or photograph a receipt. Scan results stay on your phone.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. For questions, see the support page.
